The Business Model

Why Health Data Sovereignty Changes Everything

Health systems and corporate data brokers sell anonymized patient files for billions every year without patients seeing a penny. We return power, precision treatment, and compensation to where it belongs: the patient.

Precision Clinical Trial Matching

Never get lost in medical jargon or 50-page questionnaires. Our automated engine evaluates your actual diagnostic history, HbA1c, eGFR, lipid levels, and current medications directly against ClinicalTrials.gov protocols to pinpoint the exact trials you qualify for.

Monetize Your Real-World Data

Life sciences companies pay substantial stipends for longitudinal real-world evidence (RWE). Choose to securely license your de-identified, high-fidelity health records directly to university studies and biotech sponsors — and receive 100% of the compensation.

Local-First Zero-Knowledge Vault

We never upload unencrypted protected health information (PHI) to cloud servers. All records pulled via SMART-on-FHIR are stored encrypted in your local database using your own private Fernet AES-256 key.

How Hackers & Healers Works

Take back ownership of your medical history in three frictionless steps.

01

1-Click SMART-on-FHIR Sync

Select your health system (Epic MyChart, Cerner, etc.) and authenticate using the federal SMART-on-FHIR standard. We never see or store your hospital password.

02

Instant Biomarker Evaluation

Our algorithms instantly parse your labs, active conditions, and vitals locally. We map your profile against active clinical trials and research registries without sending raw data to third parties.

03

Participate, Heal, or Get Paid

Connect with clinical investigators for cutting-edge treatments at zero cost, or opt into privacy-preserving observational studies that pay recurring stipends for your data contribution.

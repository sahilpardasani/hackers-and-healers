1. About this prototype

Nudge Lab (the “Prototype”) is an experimental, local-first demonstration that can connect, with a patient’s authorization, to a health system’s FHIR API and display selected health-record summaries. It is provided for evaluation and demonstration, not as a production healthcare service.

2. Eligibility and authorized use

Use the Prototype only if you are the patient or have authority to act for that patient, and only with permission to access the selected health-system account and records. Each patient must complete their own sign-in and authorization with the health system. Do not ask another person to give you their password, use their session, or authorize access on their behalf. The Prototype does not request or store MyChart passwords.

You agree not to use the Prototype to access records without authorization, interfere with a health system or its services, or violate applicable law or the health system’s terms.

3. Health information and local storage

In its current local-demo configuration, the Prototype communicates with the health system you select and stores OAuth tokens and retrieved FHIR records in an encrypted SQLite database on the computer running the app. The encryption key is configured locally by the operator. Anyone with access to that computer, database, or key may be able to access the records; encryption does not replace device security. Do not use a shared or untrusted computer for real health information.

The Prototype is not designed or approved for production handling of protected health information. Do not deploy it for real patients or represent that it meets HIPAA or any other regulatory requirement without a separate legal, privacy, security, and operational review.

Selecting Disconnect clears the saved OAuth connection and stops future syncs, but the current prototype does not delete records already stored in its local database. Those records remain encrypted on that computer until the database is removed. Preserve the encryption key securely if you need to read the database; losing the key can make it unrecoverable.

4. Health-system authorization and access

The health system controls authentication, the data it makes available, the scopes and duration of access, refresh-token behavior, and revocation. The Prototype cannot guarantee that access will continue or that any particular record will be returned. You can revoke the app’s authorization through the health system where that option is available; disconnecting here is not a substitute for revoking authorization there.

5. No medical advice or emergency service

The dashboard’s summaries and calculations are informational and may be incomplete, delayed, or incorrect. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for a licensed clinician’s judgment. Do not make care decisions based on the Prototype. Contact your healthcare professional for medical questions. For an emergency, call your local emergency number or seek emergency care.
